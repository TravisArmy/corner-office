CORNER OFFICE - START EVERYONE FRESH
====================================

Do this AFTER you upload the new files to GitHub.

1. Go to supabase.com and open your project.
2. In the left menu, click "SQL Editor".
3. Click "New query".
4. Copy the one line below, paste it into the box, and click "Run":

delete from auth.users;

5. Supabase may warn you that this deletes rows. That is what we want. Confirm it.

What this does:
- Deletes every account, every saved game and every leaderboard row.
- Keeps the closing-price tables from Part 2 (closes, market_days), so no market data is lost.

Afterwards:
- Everyone, including you, clicks "Create Investor" and signs up again.
  The same email addresses and investor names can be used again.
- The new game files also clear the old saved game out of each person's browser
  the first time they open the site, so nobody is left with leftovers.
